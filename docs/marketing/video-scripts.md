# KeepItAll — first 10 short videos

Ready to produce for TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. English, 9:16 vertical, 20–35 seconds each.

## How to make them

- **Visuals:** each shot below has a **Firefly prompt**. In Adobe Firefly (Generate video) set **aspect ratio 9:16**, paste the prompt, generate 2–3 options and keep the best. Firefly makes short clips (a few seconds each), so one shot = one generation.
- **Screen recordings:** shots marked **📱 SCREEN** are a recording of your phone using www.getkeepitall.com (iPhone: Control Centre → Screen Recording; Android: Quick Settings → Screen record). These are the most convincing shots; never skip them.
- **On-screen text:** add it in your editor (Adobe Express, Premiere Rush or CapCut). Don't ask Firefly to write text; AI video garbles letters.
- **Voiceover:** record it yourself or use a text-to-speech voice in your editor (British or neutral English works best for this audience).
- **Subtitles:** turn on auto-captions. Most people watch on mute.
- **First 2 seconds decide everything:** start with the hook text on screen and movement already happening.
- **End card (all videos):** "Free check: getkeepitall.com" (+ "link in bio"). Then in the caption: *Not legal advice.*

### Rules for every video (legal and platform)

- Say **"may be owed" / "you could be entitled"**, never "you will get" or "guaranteed".
- Don't name a real flight as delayed unless it really was, and never show real people's boarding passes or booking references.
- Weather, air traffic control and third-party strikes **usually don't** qualify. Be honest about it (video 6); it builds trust.
- Don't use airline logos or make the video look like it comes from an airline.

### Reusable Firefly style suffix

Add this to the end of any prompt for a consistent look:

> *vertical 9:16, realistic, natural light, shallow depth of field, handheld smartphone feel, no text, no logos*

## Publishing plan

- **Order:** start with 1, 2, 3 and 10 (pin 10). Then one a day.
- **Same video, 3 platforms:** TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts.
- **Reuse what works:** after a week, make 3 variations of the best performer (new hook, same body).
- **Reply to every comment.** "Was my flight X covered?" is a lead. Answer briefly and point to the free check.
- **Big disruption day (strikes, storms)?** Post a quick version of video 1 or 6 the same day about that event.
