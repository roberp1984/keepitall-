# Airline claim links: verification checklist

These links were found on each airline's **official website** in October 2026. All 29 links were checked by the operator on 27 October 2026 and are now used in claim packs. Re-check them every few months: airlines move their forms.

For each airline:
1. Open the link on your phone or computer.
2. Check that it is the airline's **own site** and that it leads to a way to **claim delay/cancellation compensation** (EU261/UK261), not just baggage or refunds.
3. Tick the box. If the link is wrong or broken, write what you found next to it.

When you're done, tell Claude which ones are correct; they will be marked `verified: true` in `data/airlines.json` and start appearing in claim packs and airline pages.
